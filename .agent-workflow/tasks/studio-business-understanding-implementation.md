**Outcome:**
People can understand the actual proposed customization, its simulated consequences and limits, and whether it is approved or in use, from one clear explanation in its continuing conversation.

**Target:**
The Daymark Customization Studio, collection and host-owned lifecycle presentation in keir-demo-app.

**Scope:**
Derive shared rule descriptions from checked artifacts and associated simulation summaries from existing evidence; implement the selected inline summary, before/after preview and simulation presentation; correct collection descriptions and terminology; add focused regression coverage and verify delivery in the running demo.

**Constraints:**
Keep Keir Core domain-independent. Reuse existing lifecycle endpoints and checked evidence. Preserve exact owner/revision/evidence and freshness checks, separate approval and activation, uncertainty, explicit names, conversations and unsent text.
